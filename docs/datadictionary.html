Data Dictionary

Spatial layers, coordinate reference systems, and source origins

Note

This list catalogs data required for the 12 maps and 13 episodes described in this atlas project, along with their source origins and notes regarding how they integrate into the workshop flow.

Overview & Data Preparation

Items from the Carpentry Google Drive are downloaded as a single compressed archive by scripts/data_prep_new.r (or scripts/data_prep.r). They unpack into source_data/:

  • Hillshades at 3 scales and extents:
    • Campus and the surrounding area
    • The Bight of California
    • The Western United States

Layer Catalog by Map

Map 1: Campus Overview

  • Coordinate Reference System: Aligned to campus_DEM (NAD83 / California zone 5 / EPSG:2874 or UTM).
  • Vectors:
    • Walkways & Campus Buildings: Campus_Buildings.shp (ICM via AGO circa 2017).
    • Campus Trees: UCSB Treekeeper ArcGIS Online layer.
    • Bike Paths: bikelanescollapsedv8.shp (source_data/icm_bikes/bike_paths/).
    • Wetland & Lagoon Habitats: NCOS habitat boundary shapefiles.
  • Rasters:
    • Merged bathymetry + topography: campus_bathotopo.tif
    • Hillshade raster generated via terra::shade().

Map 2: Thematic Trees & Water

  • Campus Trees (DTK_012116.shp) filtered by top 5 species and classified by height.
  • Stream and lagoon network (california_streams/streams_crop.shp).
  • Campus bikeway centerlines.

Maps 3, 4, 5 & 6: Progressive Zoom Series

  • Map 3 (Western US): Broad regional context with hillshade and elevation gradient.
  • Map 4 (California Coast): State boundary, populated places, and Channel Islands.
  • Map 5 (Bight of California): Southern California coastal curvature, Santa Barbara Channel.
  • Map 6 (UCSB & Environs): Detailed base map linking regional geometry to campus bounds.

Map 8: PlanetScope Satellite Imagery

  • 4-band and 8-band PlanetScope imagery representing seasonal snapshots (Fall, Winter, Spring, Summer).
  • Used for natural color (RGB) and false-color infrared visualization.

Map 10: Vector Intersections

  • Bike paths (bikelanescollapsedv8.shp) & California streams (streams_crop.shp).
  • Intersection points computed via terra::intersect(), saved as output_data/creek_bike_intersections.shp.

Map 11: Dibblee Geologic Quadrangle

  • Georeferenced geologic quadrangle map of Goleta (source_data/07gGoleta/DB0007.tif).
  • Rectified using terra::rectify().

Map 12: 12-Month NDVI Phenology Stack

  • Monthly PlanetScope imagery clipped to UCSB campus.
  • Normalized Difference Vegetation Index: \[\text{NDVI} = \frac{\text{NIR} - \text{Red}}{\text{NIR} + \text{Red}}\]
  • Outputs 12-month raster time series and mean NDVI seasonal profile plot.

## Layer Catalog by Map

### Map 1: Campus Overview
* **Coordinate Reference System:** Aligned to `campus_DEM` (NAD83 / California zone 5 / EPSG:2874 or UTM).
* **Vectors:**
  * Walkways & Campus Buildings: `Campus_Buildings.shp` (ICM via AGO circa 2017).
  * Campus Trees: UCSB Treekeeper ArcGIS Online layer.
  * Bike Paths: `bikelanescollapsedv8.shp` (`source_data/icm_bikes/bike_paths/`).
  * Wetland & Lagoon Habitats: NCOS habitat boundary shapefiles.
* **Rasters:**
  * Merged bathymetry + topography: `campus_bathotopo.tif`
  * Hillshade raster generated via `terra::shade()`.

### Map 2: Thematic Trees & Water
* Campus Trees (`DTK_012116.shp`) filtered by top 5 species and classified by height.
* Stream and lagoon network (`california_streams/streams_crop.shp`).
* Campus bikeway centerlines.

### Maps 3, 4, 5 & 6: Progressive Zoom Series
* **Map 3 (Western US):** Broad regional context with hillshade and elevation gradient.
* **Map 4 (California Coast):** State boundary, populated places, and Channel Islands.
* **Map 5 (Bight of California):** Southern California coastal curvature, Santa Barbara Channel.
* **Map 6 (UCSB & Environs):** Detailed base map linking regional geometry to campus bounds.

### Map 8: PlanetScope Satellite Imagery
* 4-band and 8-band PlanetScope imagery representing seasonal snapshots (Fall, Winter, Spring, Summer).
* Used for natural color (RGB) and false-color infrared visualization.

### Map 10: Vector Intersections
* Bike paths (`bikelanescollapsedv8.shp`) & California streams (`streams_crop.shp`).
* Intersection points computed via `terra::intersect()`, saved as `output_data/creek_bike_intersections.shp`.

### Map 11: Dibblee Geologic Quadrangle
* Georeferenced geologic quadrangle map of Goleta (`source_data/07gGoleta/DB0007.tif`).
* Rectified using `terra::rectify()`.

### Map 12: 12-Month NDVI Phenology Stack
* Monthly PlanetScope imagery clipped to UCSB campus.
* Normalized Difference Vegetation Index:
  $$\text{NDVI} = \frac{\text{NIR} - \text{Red}}{\text{NIR} + \text{Red}}$$
* Outputs 12-month raster time series and mean NDVI seasonal profile plot.
