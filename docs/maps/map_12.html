Publication Plate: Month-by-Month NDVI Small Multiples

The finished atlas plate below provides a publication-quality small multiples sequence of the month-by-month Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) across North Campus Open Space (NCOS) and West Campus over the 2023–2024 annual cycle. Multiple daily satellite passes within each calendar month are harmonized into monthly composites, tracking seasonal phenology from dry-season dormancy (September–November), the winter green flush (December–January), peak spring biomass in April (mean NDVI \(\sim 0.41\)), and the early-summer dry-down (May–August).

Technical Summary

  • Objective: Calculate monthly Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) rasters across an entire annual cycle using PlanetScope satellite collections to answer the question: What month is UCSB greenest?
  • Formula: \[\text{NDVI} = \frac{\text{NIR} - \text{Red}}{\text{NIR} + \text{Red}} = \frac{\text{Band 8} - \text{Band 6}}{\text{Band 8} + \text{Band 6}}\]
  • Key Packages: terra, ggplot2, dplyr, tidyr, lubridate, scales
  • Data Sources:
    • Monthly PlanetScope AnalyticMS scenes (output_data/ndvi/*.tif)
    • Campus and NCOS study area boundaries (scripts/ncos.geojson)
  • Outputs:
    • Initial raw stack verification: images/ndvi_series_stack_1.png
    • Acquisition-date faceted stack: final_output/map_12.png
    • Vegetation phenology trajectory curve: final_output/plot_12.png
    • Publication-ready monthly small multiples: final_output/map_12_monthly_ndvi.png (also archived at final_output/map_12_small_multiples.png)

Step-by-Step Cartographic Workflow & Intermediate Outputs

The script scripts/map_12.r moves from raw multispectral GeoTIFFs through batch index calculation, extent filtering, Julian date sorting, and longitudinal trend plotting.

Step 1: Batch NDVI Calculation & Dimension Harmonization

For each monthly scene, Band 8 (NIR) and Band 6 (Red) are extracted to compute NDVI. A consistency filter inspects raster dimensions (dim(test_size) == valid_tiff), discarding mismatched tiles:

# Batch NDVI formula
ndvi <- (nir - red) / (nir + red)

# Harmonize dimensions across monthly stack
ndvi_series_stack <- rast(ndvi_series_paths)

Step 2: Intermediate Plot — Initial Full-Extent Raster Stack

An initial diagnostic plot verifies that all seasonal scenes ingested successfully into the multi-layer SpatRaster object:

# Plot initial stack layers
plot(ndvi_series_stack)
ggsave("images/ndvi_series_stack_1.png", plot = last_plot(), bg = "white")

Step 3: Cropping to Focus AOI & Tidy Pivot for Faceting

The full stack is cropped to the North Campus Open Space and West Campus reserve boundary (scripts/ncos.geojson). It is then converted into a long tidy data frame (as.data.frame(xy=TRUE) %>% pivot_longer()), extracting clean 8-digit date strings (YYYYMMDD) to resolve calendar dates and Julian days of the year (yday(date)).

# Crop stack to focus area
ncos_extent <- vect("scripts/ncos.geojson")
ndvi_series_stack <- crop(ndvi_series_stack, project(ncos_extent, ndvi_series_stack))

# Convert to tidy dataframe for ggplot faceting
ndvi_series_df <- as.data.frame(ndvi_series_stack, xy = TRUE, na.rm = FALSE) %>%
  pivot_longer(-(x:y), names_to = "image_date", values_to = "NDVI_value") %>%
  mutate(
    yyyymmdd = sub(".*?([0-9]{8}).*", "\\1", image_date),
    date = as_date(yyyymmdd),
    julian_date = yday(date)
  )

Step 4: Exploratory Faceting & Phenology Trajectory Curve

  1. Acquisition Facets (map_12.png): Displays all 11 individual satellite acquisition scenes to observe sensor coverage patterns and data consistency.
  2. Phenology Curve (plot_12.png): The global spatial mean NDVI is computed per scene, revealing that April 2024 achieved the annual maximum canopy greenness.

Step 5: Publication Plate — Well-Formatted Monthly Small Multiples

To produce a cartographically refined atlas plate: * Intra-month acquisition passes are averaged into seamless monthly surfaces (group_by(x, y, year_month, month_name) %>% summarize(NDVI = mean(...))). * A standardized, diverging Red-Yellow-Green spectral ramp (scale_fill_distiller(palette = "RdYlGn", direction = 1)) maps negative values (open water and wetland channels) to deep rust, bare soils to buff/yellow, and dense canopy to rich forest green across identical limits [-0.2, 0.8]. * Distracting coordinate clutter and tick marks are eliminated in favor of clean facet headers, subtle borders, and an anchored horizontal legend.

# Render publication small multiples plate
ggplot(monthly_ndvi_df) +
  geom_raster(aes(x = x, y = y, fill = NDVI)) +
  scale_fill_distiller(
    palette = "RdYlGn", direction = 1,
    limits = c(-0.2, 0.8), oob = scales::squish
  ) +
  facet_wrap(~month_name, ncol = 5) +
  coord_equal() +
  theme_minimal()

### Publication Plate: Month-by-Month NDVI Small Multiples
The finished atlas plate below provides a **publication-quality small multiples sequence** of the month-by-month Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) across North Campus Open Space (NCOS) and West Campus over the 2023–2024 annual cycle. Multiple daily satellite passes within each calendar month are harmonized into monthly composites, tracking seasonal phenology from dry-season dormancy (September–November), the winter green flush (December–January), peak spring biomass in April (mean NDVI $\sim 0.41$), and the early-summer dry-down (May–August).

## Technical Summary

* **Objective:** Calculate monthly Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) rasters across an entire annual cycle using PlanetScope satellite collections to answer the question: *What month is UCSB greenest?*
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{NDVI} = \frac{\text{NIR} - \text{Red}}{\text{NIR} + \text{Red}} = \frac{\text{Band 8} - \text{Band 6}}{\text{Band 8} + \text{Band 6}}$$
* **Key Packages:** `terra`, `ggplot2`, `dplyr`, `tidyr`, `lubridate`, `scales`
* **Data Sources:**
  * Monthly PlanetScope AnalyticMS scenes (`output_data/ndvi/*.tif`)
  * Campus and NCOS study area boundaries (`scripts/ncos.geojson`)
* **Outputs:**
  * Initial raw stack verification: `images/ndvi_series_stack_1.png`
  * Acquisition-date faceted stack: `final_output/map_12.png`
  * Vegetation phenology trajectory curve: `final_output/plot_12.png`
  * Publication-ready monthly small multiples: `final_output/map_12_monthly_ndvi.png` (also archived at `final_output/map_12_small_multiples.png`)

## Step-by-Step Cartographic Workflow & Intermediate Outputs

The script `scripts/map_12.r` moves from raw multispectral GeoTIFFs through batch index calculation, extent filtering, Julian date sorting, and longitudinal trend plotting.

### Step 1: Batch NDVI Calculation & Dimension Harmonization

For each monthly scene, Band 8 (NIR) and Band 6 (Red) are extracted to compute NDVI. A consistency filter inspects raster dimensions (`dim(test_size) == valid_tiff`), discarding mismatched tiles:

```r
# Batch NDVI formula
ndvi <- (nir - red) / (nir + red)

# Harmonize dimensions across monthly stack
ndvi_series_stack <- rast(ndvi_series_paths)
```

### Step 2: Intermediate Plot — Initial Full-Extent Raster Stack

An initial diagnostic plot verifies that all seasonal scenes ingested successfully into the multi-layer `SpatRaster` object:

```r
# Plot initial stack layers
plot(ndvi_series_stack)
ggsave("images/ndvi_series_stack_1.png", plot = last_plot(), bg = "white")
```

![Intermediate Diagnostic: Initial full-extent multi-scene raster stack](../images/ndvi_series_stack_1.png){fig-alt="Map 12 Initial Raster Stack" width="100%"}

### Step 3: Cropping to Focus AOI & Tidy Pivot for Faceting

The full stack is cropped to the North Campus Open Space and West Campus reserve boundary (`scripts/ncos.geojson`). It is then converted into a long tidy data frame (`as.data.frame(xy=TRUE) %>% pivot_longer()`), extracting clean 8-digit date strings (`YYYYMMDD`) to resolve calendar dates and Julian days of the year (`yday(date)`).

```r
# Crop stack to focus area
ncos_extent <- vect("scripts/ncos.geojson")
ndvi_series_stack <- crop(ndvi_series_stack, project(ncos_extent, ndvi_series_stack))

# Convert to tidy dataframe for ggplot faceting
ndvi_series_df <- as.data.frame(ndvi_series_stack, xy = TRUE, na.rm = FALSE) %>%
  pivot_longer(-(x:y), names_to = "image_date", values_to = "NDVI_value") %>%
  mutate(
    yyyymmdd = sub(".*?([0-9]{8}).*", "\\1", image_date),
    date = as_date(yyyymmdd),
    julian_date = yday(date)
  )
```

### Step 4: Exploratory Faceting & Phenology Trajectory Curve

1. **Acquisition Facets (`map_12.png`):** Displays all 11 individual satellite acquisition scenes to observe sensor coverage patterns and data consistency.
2. **Phenology Curve (`plot_12.png`):** The global spatial mean NDVI is computed per scene, revealing that April 2024 achieved the annual maximum canopy greenness.

### Step 5: Publication Plate — Well-Formatted Monthly Small Multiples

To produce a cartographically refined atlas plate:
* Intra-month acquisition passes are averaged into seamless monthly surfaces (`group_by(x, y, year_month, month_name) %>% summarize(NDVI = mean(...))`).
* A standardized, diverging Red-Yellow-Green spectral ramp (`scale_fill_distiller(palette = "RdYlGn", direction = 1)`) maps negative values (open water and wetland channels) to deep rust, bare soils to buff/yellow, and dense canopy to rich forest green across identical limits `[-0.2, 0.8]`.
* Distracting coordinate clutter and tick marks are eliminated in favor of clean facet headers, subtle borders, and an anchored horizontal legend.

```r
# Render publication small multiples plate
ggplot(monthly_ndvi_df) +
  geom_raster(aes(x = x, y = y, fill = NDVI)) +
  scale_fill_distiller(
    palette = "RdYlGn", direction = 1,
    limits = c(-0.2, 0.8), oob = scales::squish
  ) +
  facet_wrap(~month_name, ncol = 5) +
  coord_equal() +
  theme_minimal()
```
